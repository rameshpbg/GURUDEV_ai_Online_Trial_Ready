## Product limits

- No true independent crop breeding dataset was submitted. Accuracy, stability and superiority on rice or other crops remain unknown.
- No Windows installer/microphone test on actual hardware in this session; launcher retained from v0.2.
- Browser UI served; no full physical speech-to-text acceptance test.
- No authentication or production multi-user infrastructure; local-only.
- No unrestricted automatic code repair, self-training LLM, permanent background AI worker or unattended field/lab actuation.
- No real HapMap/VCF, phenotype mixed-model or legacy R-package integration. Those require original source and benchmark data.
